About

I am a student who enjoys mathematics, science, and computing, and who likes turning questions from everyday life into things I can observe, test, and understand.

This site is a growing record of how I think. I document not only finished results, but also where a question began, what I tried, what changed my mind, and what I would like to investigate next. I am currently in fifth grade, and I hope this archive will continue to grow through university and beyond.

Beyond mathematics, science, and code, I also keep selected records of piano, photography, video, travel, and meaningful encounters. I am interested in finding unexpected connections between experiences that may initially seem unrelated.

Current Interests

  • Learning and routines — observing how small actions influence focus and the start of study.
  • Mathematics and patterns — finding rules and explaining a problem in more than one way.
  • Science and experiments — testing why an actual result may differ from a prediction.
  • Computing and making — learning how systems work by turning ideas into code.
