The **Trust Pink Badge** received a Silver Award at the [46th Seoul Student Science Invention Competition](https://ssei.sen.go.kr/) in 2025. The project explored how an E-Ink display and Arduino timer could communicate a badge's valid period while remaining quiet and energy efficient.
