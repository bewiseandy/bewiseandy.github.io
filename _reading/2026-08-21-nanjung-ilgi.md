## Central Question

How can short daily records help us understand leadership more accurately than a heroic story written afterward?

## What Stayed With Me

The diary records weather, health, military preparation, reports, mistakes, and personal worry. The repeated details show that leadership was not a single dramatic decision. It was the continued work of observing conditions, checking information, preparing, and accepting responsibility.

## Evidence and Interpretation

Because the entries were written close to the events, they preserve uncertainty that a later biography might remove. This makes the diary useful not only as a story about Admiral Yi, but also as evidence that must be compared with maps, official records, and historical context.

## How My Thinking Changed

Before reading, I mainly thought of Yi Sun-sin through famous victories. The diary made me pay more attention to the ordinary work behind those outcomes: repeated preparation, physical endurance, judgment under incomplete information, and concern for other people.

## Next Inquiry

I would like to choose several entries from before and after one naval battle, place them on a timeline, and compare the diary with a map and another historical source. This could show what the diary explains clearly and what it leaves unanswered.
